Confirm before you go

Restaurants join and leave the program, change their hours, and sometimes stop accepting EBT without notice. We work from public lists and update them, but a listing here is not a promise that a restaurant will accept your card on the day you visit. Call ahead when you can. Every listing with a phone number has a call button.
