Accessibility

Using this site with a screen reader, keyboard, or large text

Last updated November 8, 2026

The people who need this site most are often older, disabled, or using an old phone on a slow connection. Accessibility is not a nice-to-have here; it is the point.

Our target

We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA. That is a goal, not a certification: the site has not yet been through an independent audit, and this page will say so until it has.

What the site does

  • Every restaurant on the map is also in a plain list below it, in reading order.
  • Fields have real labels, and status messages are announced when they change.
  • Everything works with a keyboard, with a visible focus ring on every control.
  • Text uses Atkinson Hyperlegible, designed for low vision, and scales with browser zoom.
  • Animations and smooth scrolling stop when your system asks for reduced motion.
  • Colour is never the only way to tell boroughs apart; each also has a name.
  • Tap targets are large, and the layout works in one column on a phone.

Known limitations

  • The map is visual. Pins, borough shapes, and the pan-and-zoom view are hard or impossible to use without sight. The list is the equivalent path, and it is complete.
  • Directions leave the site. The directions button opens Google Maps, which we do not control and cannot make accessible.
  • The map is drawn by hand. Place names and shapes are simplified and are not a survey-accurate reference.
  • No independent audit yet. The checks so far are our own, plus keyboard and screen-reader testing by the maintainer.

Tell us what is broken

If something stops you from finding a meal, that is a bug worth fixing quickly. Write to hello@snapmapnyc.com with the page, what you were trying to do, and what your browser or assistive technology is. We aim to reply within five business days.

If you need a different format

The restaurant list can be shared as a plain text or spreadsheet file on request. Ask at the address above.
