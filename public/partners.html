Snap Map NYC is a free website that shows the New York City restaurants where people with SNAP benefits can buy a hot, ready-to-eat meal through the Restaurant Meals Program.

The gap

The Restaurant Meals Program lets some SNAP recipients, including people who are 60 or older, have a disability, or are experiencing homelessness, buy prepared food with an EBT card. Many eligible New Yorkers have never heard of it, and the official list is a document, not something you can use while standing on a corner deciding where to eat.

The people who most need this are the least likely to install an app, make an account, or tolerate a slow page on an old phone. That is what this site is built for.

What it is

  • A hand-drawn map of the city with a pin for every participating restaurant.
  • Search by address, ZIP code, or current location, sorted nearest first.
  • Browse by borough, or search by restaurant name.
  • Works in a browser with no install, no sign-up, and no cookies.
  • Every spot also appears in a plain list, for keyboard and screen-reader users.

Who it reaches

Directly: SNAP recipients who are older, disabled, or unhoused, and their families. Indirectly: the people they ask for help, including caseworkers, librarians, hospital discharge planners, pantry volunteers, and 311 staff.

What support pays for

Level What it pays for Per year
Partner A full verification pass over all 235 listings against the current state list, plus a named credit on the site. $500
Borough Verification and outreach for one borough, translated handout cards for partner sites, and the aggregate report for that borough. $2,500
Sustaining A year of maintenance: verification, accessibility fixes, translation, printed materials, and monthly reporting. $5,000

Partial support and in-kind help are welcome. If your organization already maintains food access data, sharing it may be worth more than money.

What money never buys

Sponsorship does not buy placement, ranking, the inclusion or removal of a restaurant, or any say over what the site says. Listings are not for sale. The sponsorship policy says this in full, so you can check it before and after you give.

What you get back

A monthly aggregate report: visits, address and location lookups, directions clicks, and call clicks, plus the boroughs and ZIP codes where the site is used. Nothing about an individual visitor is collected, so nothing about an individual can be reported.

Independence

Snap Map NYC is not run by the City or State and is not affiliated with the USDA or NYS OTDA. Funding does not change that sentence, and it will stay on the site.

Talk to us

Write to hello@snapmapnyc.com with your organization, what you would like to support, and any reporting you need. A short call is usually enough to work out the rest.
