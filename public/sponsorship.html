The fine print

Sponsorship and editorial independence

Last updated November 8, 2026

Snap Map NYC is free to use and always will be. It stays online because organizations that care about food access help pay for the work of keeping it accurate.

What sponsorship is

Sponsorship is financial support for the ongoing running of the site. It pays for things like checking listings against the current state list, fixing wrong addresses, translating copy, printing cards that partner organizations can hand out, and keeping the domain and hosting running. It is not a purchase of space, and it is not a donation to a charity.

What a sponsor gets

  • Their name and logo in a clearly labelled sponsor section, and a link if they want one.
  • A short, plain description of what they do, subject to our approval.
  • An aggregate report: visits, address and location lookups, directions clicks, and call clicks. Never anything about an individual visitor.
  • Acknowledgement in printed partner materials, if they are part of that program.

What money never buys

  • Placement, ranking, or priority in the list or on the map.
  • Inclusion of a restaurant, or removal of one.
  • Any change to what the site says about the Restaurant Meals Program.
  • Approval over editorial content, design, or which listings exist.
  • Any information about the people who use the site. We do not have any to give.

How sponsorship is disclosed

Sponsors are listed on the site with the word "sponsor," and sponsored material is labelled where it appears. A listing is never marked as sponsored, because listings are not for sale.

Current sponsors

None yet. When there are, they will be named here. Until then, the site is paid for by the person who maintains it.

Who we turn down

We may decline money from an organization whose business conflicts with the purpose of the site, or whose involvement would mislead people about the Restaurant Meals Program or about any government agency's involvement. We do not accept sponsorship that comes with conditions on what the site may say.

Government and nonprofit partners

Some support arrives as a grant or a contract rather than a sponsorship. Those arrangements are disclosed the same way, with the funder named and the purpose stated. Accepting public money does not make this an official City or State service, and the site will keep saying so.

No affiliation

Sponsorship by an agency, a hospital, a health plan, or a nonprofit does not mean that the organization runs this site, endorses its contents, or speaks through it. Snap Map NYC is independent, and remains independent.

Questions

Sponsorship questions: hello@snapmapnyc.com. The one-page summary for partners is at partners.html.
