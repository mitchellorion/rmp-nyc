Privacy notice

What happens to your information

Last updated November 8, 2026

The short version. No account. No cookies. No sign-up. No newsletter. We never sell or share your information. There is no profile of you to build, because the site does not keep one.

What stays in your browser

  • What you type. An address or ZIP code you type stays in the page until you press "Find spots near me." You can clear it at any time with the clear button.
  • Your location. If you press "Use my location," your browser asks for permission. If you allow it, your coordinates are used inside the page to sort the list by distance. They are not sent to us, and they are not stored.
  • The state of the page. Your borough and address are kept in the page's own address bar (for example ?borough=Brooklyn) so that a link reopens the same view. That is part of the URL, not a file on your device, and it is only shared if you share the link.

The site does not use cookies, localStorage, or any other storage on your device.

What leaves your browser

Address lookups go to OpenStreetMap

The site cannot look up an address on its own. When you press "Find spots near me" and the text is not a ZIP code already on the list, the text is sent as part of a web request to Nominatim, the free address search run by the OpenStreetMap Foundation at nominatim.openstreetmap.org. That request includes the address you typed and your IP address, and the Foundation's own privacy policy applies to it.

We do not keep a copy of the address and we do not log it. If the text is a ZIP code that already appears in the list, the answer is calculated in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.

The site itself is hosted by GitHub Pages

Like almost every website, the server that delivers the page records standard technical information, including your IP address, in its logs. That is handled by GitHub under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Analytics, when it is switched on

The site can use GoatCounter to count visits. It is cookieless: it sets nothing on your device, builds no identifier, and does not follow you to other sites. What it records is aggregate: which page was viewed, roughly where the request came from, the referring site, and a handful of action names such as nearby-lookup, directions, and call.

Those action names are the only thing we add. No address, no ZIP code, no restaurant name, and no search term is ever sent to the analytics service.

If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, the analytics script is not loaded at all, so nothing is counted.

What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your information with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not build a profile of you or track you across other websites.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or EBT card number.
  • We do not show ads.

Children

The site is meant for adults looking for a meal, and it is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because the site does not store personal information, there is usually nothing to access, correct, or delete. If you believe we hold something about you, write to us and we will look and respond. New York residents have rights under the SHIELD Act, and California residents have rights under the CCPA and CPRA, including the right to know and the right to delete. We do not sell or share personal information as those laws define it.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top changes too. We will not quietly broaden what we collect; if we ever did, we would say so here in plain language.
